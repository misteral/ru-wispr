# Dikto — brand positioning

- **Status:** draft
- **Date:** 2026-03-31

## Decision

We choose **Dikto** as the product name for the Russian market.

Russian display form: **Дикто**  
Primary written brand form: **Dikto**

## Why this name

**Dikto** has the best balance of clarity and brandability:

- directly associated with dictation;
- short and easy to remember;
- easy to pronounce in Russian;
- works well in Latin for domain, app, and marketing assets;
- sounds like a product name, not a generic feature.

## Core positioning

**Russian dictation for Mac without the cloud.**

Expanded version:

> Dikto is a Russian voice dictation app for Mac that actually works.
> Press a hotkey, speak naturally, and text appears where you are typing.
> Everything runs locally on your Mac — no cloud, no account, no data leaving the device.

## Core slogan options

Primary slogan:

> **Русская диктовка для Mac без облака**

Additional options:

- **Dikto. Сказал — готово.**
- **Печатайте голосом в любом приложении на Mac.**
- **Говорите там, где обычно печатаете.**

## Hero copy draft

### Variant A

**Русская диктовка для Mac, которая реально работает**

Нажмите горячую клавишу, говорите — текст появляется в Telegram, почте, браузере, документах и IDE. Всё локально, без облака.

### Variant B

**Печатайте голосом в любом приложении на Mac**

Dikto превращает речь в текст там, где вы уже работаете. Без аккаунта, без отправки данных, с упором на русский язык.

## Key marketing messages

- **Works in any app** — Telegram, email, browser, docs, chats, IDE.
- **Built for Russian speech** — strong recognition for the main target audience.
- **Local and private** — voice and text stay on the Mac.
- **One hotkey, instant workflow** — press, speak, release.
- **Faster than typing** — ideal for messages, drafts, prompts, and notes.

## Messaging angles to borrow from the market

Based on positioning used by Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, EdgeWhisper, Spokenly, Stenotype, SpeakFlow, VoxFusion, Voice Anywhere, and Voicely.

### 1. Sell the outcome, not the AI

The strongest message is not “AI dictation,” but:

- speak → text appears;
- works in any app;
- faster than typing;
- private and local;
- one hotkey.

### 2. Emphasize the Russian-language pain point

Good angle for the landing page:

> Standard Mac dictation does not handle Russian well enough.
> Dikto is the simple alternative that focuses on one thing: reliable Russian dictation.

### 3. Use concrete scenarios instead of abstract promises

Show recognizable use cases:

- replying in Telegram;
- writing emails;
- typing in browser forms;
- working in docs and notes;
- dictating prompts into ChatGPT / Claude.

### 4. Repeat “works in any app” throughout the page

This is one of the highest-converting ideas in the category.

### 5. Lead with privacy for the Russian market

Recommended phrasing:

- everything is processed directly on your Mac;
- voice and text do not go to the cloud;
- no server, no account, no tracking.

## What not to copy

- avoid overloading the promise with too many AI features;
- avoid claims like “100% accuracy”;
- avoid overly abstract English-first branding for the Russian market.
